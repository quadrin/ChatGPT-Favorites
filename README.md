Favorite chat titles and messages in ChatGPT!

Works for chat titles

Download the repo and follow the steps from this page: https://ui.vision/howto/install-chrome-extension-from-file


## Read favorites offline

Favorites are saved inside the extension, so you can read them with no Wi-Fi.

- When you star a message, the extension saves its text.
- When you star a chat, the extension downloads the full chat. A progress box in the bottom-right corner of ChatGPT shows "Saving favorites for offline: 1 of 3".
- Click the extension icon to see your saved chats and messages. This page works offline. Click "Open in tab" for a larger view.
- Click "Download favorited chats now" to get the newest version of each starred chat. ChatGPT must be open in a tab. The progress bar updates while the chats download.
